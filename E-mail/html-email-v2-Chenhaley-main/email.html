Going out to Lunch UPDATE

With the first marking period having ended, I wanted to let you know that all students (including 9th graders) eligible To Go Out To Lunch based on the first marking period will be eligible next Thursday, October 24. Teachers will be submitting their grades and we should everything processed by that date. You might be eligible right now, but NOT eligible based on the first marking period – this will also take effect next Thursday, October 24 as well. I want to remind you the requirements To Go Out to Lunch Here are the requirements:

  • 90% Attendance in Every Class
  • 80% On Time in Every Class
  • Passing All Classes
  • No Peace Center Issues in the previous marking period
  • Necessary Paperwork Submitted via Operoo (Emergency Contact Form / Going Out To Lunch Permission Slip / Lunch Application)
